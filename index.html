How to play

Open Ingenium, go to How to Play, and run the tutorial. Tap a tile to rotate it 90°. Power flows from the lightning generators until every bulb is lit. The board wraps: top joins bottom, left joins right.

No account, no ads

Ingenium is free. There are no in-app purchases and no advertisements. Game Center is optional and only used if you sign in to Apple for leaderboards.
