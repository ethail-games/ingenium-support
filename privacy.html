Privacy Policy

Ingenium · EthAil · Last updated: 5 September 2026

Ingenium does not require an account and does not show ads. We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking, or the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Data on the device

Completed levels, scores, settings, and language are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to our servers. We do not operate a game server.

Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your scores may be sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards (ingenium.level.XX and ingenium.overall). Apple processes Game Center player ID, nickname, and scores under Apple’s privacy policy. If you do not use Game Center, Ingenium does not send anything off the device.

apple.com/legal/privacy

Children

The game is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Game Center is managed by Apple and by the device’s Screen Time / Apple ID settings.
